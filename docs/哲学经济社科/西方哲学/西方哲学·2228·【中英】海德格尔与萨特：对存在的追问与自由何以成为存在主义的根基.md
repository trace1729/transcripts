看过《终局》的人会理解存在主义的困境。我们坐在这里等待某事发生，也许会发生，也许不会。但即使发生了，我们也无法理解它。或者会有多少理解者就有多少种理解。问题在于我们陷入了一种主观性，几乎否定了交流的可能性。

The communication that existentialist novels and existentialist plays usually engage in is in fact a sort of poetic communication. What's important about the kind of poetry, and that's what I would call it, that they produce is more the connotations than the denotations. Logical positivists want to free us from the ambiguity of language. The existentialists bask in it. What they want us to do is to become heroic, even if we can't become perfect, if we can't become rational, if we can't become Socratic in that respect. Or perhaps what Socrates leads to in this century is the kind of valuation of ambiguity, which says the best we can do is at least bravely confront the uncertainty that we have around us.

存在主义小说和戏剧通常进行的交流实际上是一种诗意的交流。他们创作的那种诗（我会这么称呼它）重要的是内涵而非外延。逻辑实证主义者想让我们摆脱语言的歧义，存在主义者则沉浸其中。他们希望我们变得英勇，即使我们无法变得完美、无法变得理性、无法在这方面成为苏格拉底式的人物。或者，也许苏格拉底在这个世纪所导向的，正是对歧义的重视，它说我们所能做的最好的事，就是至少勇敢地面对周围的不确定性。

And the reason why this is derived from the 19th century is worth your consideration. It seems to me that there is a connection between this tendency in 20th century Western thought and the romanticism of the early 19th century. Think of the sort of themes that come in during the early 19th century. The heroic individual fighting against the mass, the hatred of science and technology, the rejection of logic and reason and certainty, in favor of sentiment, in favor of emotion, in favor of intuition, in favor of subjectivity. If romantic literature and romantic poetry and romantic music and romantic art emphasize subjectivity, emphasize heroism, emphasize uncertainty, emphasize the lack or the limitations of human rationality, what existentialism does in the 20th century is revive that tendency with a vengeance.

为什么这源于19世纪，值得你们思考。在我看来，20世纪西方思想中的这一倾向与19世纪早期的浪漫主义之间存在联系。想想19世纪早期出现的那些主题：英雄个体对抗大众，对科学和技术的仇恨，拒绝逻辑、理性和确定性，转而支持情感、情绪、直觉和主观性。如果浪漫主义文学、诗歌、音乐和艺术强调主观性、英雄主义、不确定性，强调人类理性的缺乏或局限，那么20世纪的存在主义就是以报复性的姿态复兴了这一倾向。

And that accounts, I think, at least in part, for why the Romantic era, the first half of the 19th century, is so noteworthy for its outstanding artistic production, and yet it produces very little in the realm of science or rational inquiry. It's not a great age for science. On the other hand, the late part of the 19th century in fact does something like that, moves away from subjectivity towards science and objectivity.

我认为，这至少部分解释了为什么浪漫主义时代（19世纪上半叶）以其杰出的艺术成就而著称，但在科学或理性探究领域却产出甚少。那不是一个科学的伟大时代。另一方面，19世纪后期实际上做了类似的事情，从主观性转向科学和客观性。

When that collapses, due in large part to the work of Friedrich Nietzsche, that great titan of subjectivity, what we get left with is a revival of romanticism. Be heroic if you can't be certain. Be anguished if you can't be smug. At least do something interesting. Don't evade the question. The question is, how can I become a real human individual?

当这种转向崩溃时（很大程度上归功于弗里德里希·尼采，那位伟大的主观性巨人），我们剩下的就是浪漫主义的复兴。如果不能确定，那就英勇；如果不能自满，那就痛苦。至少做点有趣的事。不要回避问题。问题是：我如何成为一个真正的人类个体？

And isn't that what somebody like Dickens demands that we do? Sentiment over science, feeling over reason. In fact, the despair about the reasoning capacities of human beings is what is most characteristic of the literary and philosophical trend we call existentialism. And if, in fact, it isn't entirely satisfactory as a set of answers, because, in fact, it's too vague and nebulous, the questions that it asks of us are worthwhile and if they don't ultimately make you better, at least the attempt to deal with these questions will make you no worse. And if it deepens your soul without costing you a conception of objectivity and the real world, then you can both avoid solipsism and avoid being a philistine. You can actually be a real human being.

这不正是狄更斯这样的人要求我们做的吗？情感胜过科学，感受胜过理性。事实上，对人类推理能力的绝望正是我们称之为存在主义的文学和哲学思潮最显著的特征。而且，如果它作为一套答案并不完全令人满意（因为它确实太模糊、太朦胧），它向我们提出的问题是有价值的，即使这些问题最终不能让你变得更好，至少尝试处理这些问题不会让你变得更糟。如果它深化了你的灵魂，而没有让你失去对客观性和真实世界的概念，那么你就能既避免唯我论，又避免成为庸人。你实际上可以成为一个真正的人。

What the existentialist demand that we do is that we live up to the limited, inadequate, and unsatisfactory potentials of being a human being in an age of science and a world of machines. If it's not entirely satisfactory as a philosophy, it is at least a significant and sincere cry of the heart. It is a lament of exasperation at the difficulties in being a person in a world that is almost entirely depersonalized. It's an advocacy of the poetic over the rational and this is a tendency of philosophy and culture because it's a tendency in the human psyche. And until we develop the mind of God and the certainty of God, the possibility and the importance of this tendency is going to be with us.

存在主义要求我们做的是，在科学时代和机器世界中，活出作为人类那有限、不足且不尽如人意的潜能。如果它作为哲学并不完全令人满意，它至少是心灵发出的重要而真诚的呼喊。它是对在一个几乎完全非人化的世界中做人难处的愤慨悲叹。它倡导诗意而非理性，这是哲学和文化的一种倾向，因为它是人类心灵中的一种倾向。在我们拥有上帝的心智和上帝的确定性之前，这种倾向的可能性和重要性将与我们同在。
