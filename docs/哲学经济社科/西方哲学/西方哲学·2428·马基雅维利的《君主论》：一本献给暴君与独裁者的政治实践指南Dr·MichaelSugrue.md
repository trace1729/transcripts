It is always tempting for human beings to take the easy way out and decide that they're going to be meat, that they're going to give up the attempt to kindle the divine spark, which is what Marcus Aurelius called the soul, called the disposition to moral virtue. Machiavelli doesn't want to climb what Plato called the ladder of beauty in the symposium because he finds the world ugly.

马基雅维利……就此而言，他既是一位古代政治理论家，同时又是一位现代政治理论家。他在政治上并不代表什么新东西，而是一种永远存在的诱惑。人类总是容易走捷径，决定自己要做肉，放弃点燃神圣火花的尝试——马可·奥勒留称之为灵魂，即道德德性的倾向。马基雅维利不想攀登柏拉图在《会饮篇》中所说的美的阶梯，因为他发现这个世界是丑陋的。

violent and evil, and he likes it. So, in other words, not only does he know that he's in the cave, but he thinks that any attempt to move out of the cave is a kind of letting down of nature. It's an attempt to move away from nature towards some sort of chimerical, poetic, I know not what. And Machiavelli is a patron saint of all politicians who would be exclusively and immorally practical.

暴力和邪恶，而且他喜欢这样。所以，换句话说，他不仅知道自己身处洞穴之中，而且认为任何试图走出洞穴的尝试都是对自然的一种辜负。这是一种试图远离自然，走向某种虚幻的、诗意的、我不知道是什么的东西的尝试。马基雅维利是所有那些只讲实际、不讲道德的政客的守护神。

### 10. Final Evaluation: Machiavelli's Legacy, Limitations, and Ours to Choose (最终评价：马基雅维利的遗产、局限与我们的选择)

One of the great difficulties in evaluating Machiavelli is to give him his due. In other words, to be intellectually fair to him because he is a great genius. There's no way that we can honestly take that away from him. But it's also a mistake to say that while he's a great genius, we can, in practice, use this as a guide to life. If people were to take this seriously, and although God help us, some of us do, it would be a disaster for the social structure and it would be a disaster for politics. The difficulty is that we seem to vacillate between one and the other. When the better angels of our natures take over, we can see how Marcus Aurelius is a fine politician and how Plato offers us something real.

评价马基雅维利的一大困难在于要给予他应有的评价。换句话说，要在理智上对他公平，因为他是一位伟大的天才。我们无法诚实地否认这一点。但如果说他虽然是个天才，我们实际上却可以将其作为生活指南来使用，那也是错误的。如果人们认真对待这一点——虽然上帝保佑我们，我们中有些人确实如此——那将对社会结构造成灾难，对政治造成灾难。困难在于我们似乎在这两者之间摇摆不定。当我们天性中更好的天使占据上风时，我们能看到马可·奥勒留是多么优秀的政治家，柏拉图为我们提供了多么真实的东西。

solid, substantial in organizing our emotions, organizing our lives, organizing our standards of judgment. The problem is that we tend to vacillate back and forth. Every once in a while, when we think nobody's looking, we have a sneaking suspicion that Machiavelli may be right. Let him who is without sin cast the first stone. I think every one of us has done stuff that we knew was wrong at one time or another. Machiavelli is saying, I wish to liberate you from the guilt of thinking that that is a mistake. Your mistake is in not doing that all the time. Do not succumb to the temptation to be an angel. You have no chance of doing that. You are meat.

坚实而厚重，在于组织我们的情感、组织我们的生活、组织我们的判断标准。问题在于我们往往摇摆不定。偶尔，当我们以为没人注意时，会隐隐怀疑马基雅维利或许是对的。让你们中间没有罪的人先扔石头。我想我们每个人都曾做过明知不对的事。马基雅维利说：我希望把你们从那种认为这是错误的负罪感中解放出来。你们的错误在于没有一直这样做。不要屈服于成为天使的诱惑。你们没有可能做到。你们是肉。

You are meet with a rational soul, but your rational soul is nothing that glows in the dark. It's nothing metaphysical. It's just the rational part of you that allows you to decide how to best satisfy your irrationally developed desires. So Machiavelli is a kind of standing temptation. He is a great political genius, and we must give the devil his due, almost literally speaking.

你们是有理性灵魂的肉，但你们的理性灵魂并非在黑暗中发光的东西。它不是什么形而上的存在。它只是你们身上理性的部分，让你们决定如何最好地满足那些非理性发展出来的欲望。因此，马基雅维利是一种持续的诱惑。他是一位伟大的政治天才，我们必须给予魔鬼应得的评价，几乎字面意义上如此。

But at the same time, we have to understand the limitations of this. Limitation number one is that it prevents us from being what we really are, which is social animals. A Machiavellian would be unfit as a subordinate and would be unfit as a superior. Nobody in his right mind would work for Machiavelli or have Machiavelli work for him. Number two, it is a denigration in some respects of human nature.

但与此同时，我们必须理解其局限。局限之一在于，它阻止我们成为我们真正的样子，即社会性动物。一个马基雅维利式的人既不适合做下属，也不适合做上级。任何心智正常的人都不会为马基雅维利工作，也不会让马基雅维利为自己工作。其二，它在某些方面是对人性的贬低。

It is a cynical analysis of what people have done in worst-case circumstances. It is almost an entirely hopeless philosophy. By rejecting the Christian virtues of faith, hope, and charity, well, I suppose we might get by without charity. I suppose in this case we might get by without faith. But the problem with this philosophy that I think even bothers those who acknowledge its brilliance is that it is entirely hopeless philosophy. What can we expect from the next government? The same thing we can expect from this government, which is that it will be rapacious, that it will be treacherous, that it will be evil, and that it will be powerful, and that it will dominate us.

这是对人们在最坏情况下所作所为的一种犬儒式分析。这几乎是一种全然无望的哲学。通过拒绝基督教的美德——信、望、爱——好吧，我想没有爱我们也许能过，没有信我们也许也能过。但在我看来，这种哲学的问题，甚至那些承认其卓越的人也会困扰的是，它完全是一种无望的哲学。我们对下一届政府能期待什么？和我们对这届政府能期待的一样：它将贪婪、背信弃义、邪恶、强大，并且将统治我们。

The only way out of that merry-go-round is to dominate everyone else. Nature, red in fang and claw, enjoins us to make a meal of our own, enjoins us to become the wolf among the sheep. And if we have both the inclination and the unwillingness to philosophize in the platonic sense, then it seems to me that the only logical conclusion is the one that Machiavelli draws. So if you wish to go for the strictly physical, strictly anti-metaphysical politics, we're going to have a hard time connecting politics and ethics.

摆脱这个旋转木马的唯一出路是统治其他所有人。自然，血红的牙和爪，命令我们自相残杀，命令我们成为羊群中的狼。如果我们既倾向于又不愿意以柏拉图的方式去哲学化，那么在我看来，唯一合乎逻辑的结论就是马基雅维利得出的那个。因此，如果你想要严格物理的、严格反形而上学的政治，我们将很难把政治与伦理联系起来。

One of the great achievements of Plato's Republic is that politics turns out to be ethics writ large. That what is good for the soul of the individual man, the organization of the reasoning, the spirited and the desiring parts, it also turns out to be isomorphically good for the society. Because we will organize the rulers, the rational folks, at the top of society. We will have the guardians or auxiliaries, the spirited part, and we'll have the bronze people at the bottom, getting as many of their desires satisfied as possible. For Plato, there's a one-to-one connection between politics and ethics. That will be true of all the metaphysical thinkers. Also be true of the Christians.

柏拉图《理想国》的伟大成就之一在于，政治被证明是放大了的伦理。对个体灵魂有益的东西——理性、激情和欲望部分的组织——也被证明对社会同构地有益。因为我们将把统治者，即理性之人，置于社会顶端。我们将有护卫者或辅助者，即激情部分，而青铜之人将在底层，尽可能满足他们的欲望。对柏拉图而言，政治与伦理之间有一一对应的联系。所有形而上学家都会如此认为。基督徒也是如此。

On the other hand, if one wishes to adopt the single world, entirely physicalistic interpretation of human life and of ontology and of politics, of necessity, there will be a disjunction between politics and ethics. We will hear this again when we deal with David Hume's theory of justice. So if you wish politics to be moral, if you complain that politicians take too many bribes and cut too many corners and are unwilling to do what they ought to do and meet their moral obligations, you are implicitly making an argument which is founded on some sort of metaphysical conception, no matter how inchoate. You might as well fess up to it now. You're all metaphysical believers.

另一方面，如果一个人希望采纳对人生、本体论和政治的单一世界、完全物理主义的解释，那么政治与伦理之间必然会出现脱节。当我们处理大卫·休谟的正义理论时，我们会再次听到这一点。因此，如果你希望政治是道德的，如果你抱怨政客收受太多贿赂、走太多捷径、不愿做他们该做的事、不愿履行道德义务，那么你就在隐含地提出一个基于某种形而上学观念（无论多么不成熟）的论证。你不如现在就承认吧。你们都是形而上学的信徒。

If you don't wish to be a metaphysical believer, that's another possibility. Be careful you don't move down the slippery slope to Machiavellianism, because we move from the state of society to the state of nature, and the nature that Machiavelli has destined for us will be worse than any hell, because it will be immediate and tangible, and there's no way around it. It's a necessary element in the human condition. Machiavelli offers us a secular substitute for salvation.

如果你不想成为形而上学的信徒，那是另一种可能。小心不要滑向马基雅维利主义的滑坡，因为我们会从社会状态滑向自然状态，而马基雅维利为我们注定的那种自然状态将比任何地狱都更糟糕，因为它将是直接而切实的，且无处可逃。它是人类境况中的必然要素。马基雅维利为我们提供了一种世俗的救赎替代品。

Machiavelli offers us a chance for this worldly gratification. And since there's no other world for us to go to, no final judgment of God, no ultimate moral order, this is the best that the human species can attain. The Homeric virtues, the military virtues, the treacherous political virtues, those elements of Roman history which are the most disgraceful and the most appalling, Machiavelli wishes to raise those to the status of universal human felicity. An unusual, well perhaps not an unusual, a necessary but lamentable temptation. And insofar as we wish to avoid that, we must go back and think about the idea of politics and ethics.

马基雅维利为我们提供了现世满足的机会。既然没有另一个世界可去，没有上帝的最终审判，没有终极的道德秩序，这就是人类物种所能达到的最好状态。荷马式的美德、军事美德、背信弃义的政治美德，那些罗马历史中最可耻、最骇人的元素，马基雅维利希望将它们提升为普遍的人类幸福。一种不寻常的，也许并非不寻常的，但却是必要而可悲的诱惑。而就我们希望避免这一点而言，我们必须回去思考政治与伦理的观念。

The implications that it holds for ontology, because it implies a metaphysics. The implications that metaphysics have for our conception of the rest of philosophy and the way in which knowledge holds ethics, virtue, and human experience together. The way it connects our conception of the individual soul and political order and the way in which our own lives would be influenced by a decision to either succumb to the lure of this world or to take the chance that another better one awaits us. It is not a decidable proposition. Machiavelli wishes to offer us one possible solution to this set of intellectual difficulties.

它对本体论的影响，因为它暗示了一种形而上学。形而上学对我们其余哲学观念的影响，以及知识如何将伦理、德性和人类经验结合在一起。它如何将我们对个体灵魂和政治秩序的观念联系起来，以及我们自己的生活将如何受到决定的影响：是屈服于这个世界的诱惑，还是冒险等待另一个更好的世界。这不是一个可判定的命题。马基雅维利希望为我们提供一套智力难题的一种可能解决方案。
