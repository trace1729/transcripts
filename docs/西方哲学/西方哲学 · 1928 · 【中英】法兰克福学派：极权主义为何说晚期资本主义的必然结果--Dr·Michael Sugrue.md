### 5. Freud, Heidegger, and the Early Marx (弗洛伊德、海德格尔与早期马克思)

Now, one of the important twists that the Frankfurt School adds is that they see that there are gaps in Marx, and Marx by himself is not sufficient. In particular, Marxism is missing a kind of social psychology. Marx very rarely talks about the individual psyche and the individual soul and how the individual relates to society, except to say that he's pushed around by the means of production and by the ideology that accompanies that.

现在，法兰克福学派所增添的一个重要转折在于，他们看到了马克思理论中的空白，仅靠马克思自身并不足够。特别是，马克思主义缺少一种社会心理学。马克思很少谈论个体心理、个体灵魂以及个体与社会的关系，除了说个体被生产方式及其伴随的意识形态所驱使。

What the Frankfurt School does is they do a very careful reading of Freud, and they play with Freudian themes in such a way so they make a very interesting meshing of Freudian social psychology and Marxian philosophy of history. So it's a combination of Freud and Marx, which offers very many insights because in many respects these thinkers are quite complementary. Certainly Freud left out, I mean, didn't spend much time on social theory or philosophy of history. They complement each other quite nicely.

法兰克福学派所做的是对弗洛伊德进行非常细致的阅读，并以一种有趣的方式运用弗洛伊德的主题，从而将弗洛伊德的社会心理学与马克思的历史哲学巧妙地结合起来。因此，这是弗洛伊德与马克思的结合，提供了许多洞见，因为在许多方面，这两位思想家是互补的。当然，弗洛伊德确实忽略了社会理论或历史哲学，没有花太多时间在这些方面。他们彼此很好地互补。

And in addition to Freud, who offers us things like the social psychology and the connection between the individual and society, they also add in Heidegger, very important. Herbert Marcuse, one of the leading lights of the Frankfurt School, was one of Heidegger's PhD students. In other words, he got his degree under Heidegger. And while Marcuse is still a young man, Heidegger becomes a Nazi.

除了弗洛伊德（他提供了社会心理学以及个体与社会之间的联系）之外，他们还加入了海德格尔，这一点非常重要。赫伯特·马尔库塞，法兰克福学派的领军人物之一，曾是海德格尔的博士生。换句话说，他是在海德格尔指导下获得学位的。而当马尔库塞还年轻时，海德格尔成为了纳粹。

Marcuse thinks to himself, my God, this is terrible. What terrible thing has happened to the minds of our philosophers that they are actually willing to buy into this? 马尔库塞心想，天哪，这太可怕了。我们的哲学家们究竟发生了什么可怕的事情，以至于他们真的愿意接受这一切？显然，他对自己的老师感到极度失望，并且他们之间确实有足够的决裂。马尔库塞试图追问的是，哲学怎么会陷入如此可怕的境地？像海德格尔这样才华横溢的伟大思想家，怎么会穿上纳粹的制服，在讲课前行纳粹礼？这怎么可能？我们是如何达到这种危机的？

In addition to Heidegger's political preferences, what carries over into the Frankfurt School is two very important things. One, what I would call technophobia. Heidegger has a neurosis about technology. Any of you who have read that essay on the question concerning technology, he's a real technology basher. He's part of that humanistic set of humanistic intellectuals that does not like science and particularly does not like technology. You'll be part of that crowd that says it alienates you and you don't get in touch with your real being or your real self because of all these machines. And this is, I think, one of the most lamentable tendencies in 20th century philosophy.

除了海德格尔的政治倾向之外，法兰克福学派还继承了两个非常重要的东西。其一，我称之为技术恐惧症。海德格尔对技术有一种神经质的焦虑。你们中那些读过《关于技术的问题》那篇文章的人都知道，他是一个真正的技术抨击者。他是那种人文主义知识分子群体中的一员，不喜欢科学，尤其不喜欢技术。你会成为那种说技术使你异化、让你无法接触真实存在或真实自我的人群中的一员。我认为这是20世纪哲学中最令人遗憾的倾向之一。

And in addition to technophobia, another thing that's borrowed from Heidegger is a hatred of mass man. For all its posturing about universality and about extending participatory democracy and about liberation, the Frankfurt School is one of the most elitist conceptual movements of the 20th century. It appears to be accessible only to German philosophers and a few people who are professional thinkers in the post-capitalist world. It has not...

获得大量的追随者。当它成为一场群众运动时，比如60年代的学生反抗，那已经是法兰克福学派的一个淡化版本。大多数人不会有足够的心理或智力条件来真正采纳这种姿态。因此，这里暗示法兰克福学派憎恨大众，憎恨大众文化，憎恨现代发达民主国家中大多数人的文化生活。其理念是，他们被迫与世界建立一种虚幻的关系，而哲学家的任务就是尽可能解释如何飞出捕蝇瓶，如何从这种意识形态中解放出来。

我想说，对于你们中那些熟悉马克思主义传统和马克思主义哲学史的人来说，如果你们了解格奥尔格·卢卡奇的著作，特别是《历史与阶级意识》，以及安东尼奥·葛兰西的早期著作，那么法兰克福学派加上葛兰西和卢卡奇，就是试图拒绝晚期马克思，拒绝《资本论》和《大纲》中晚期实证主义的马克思。他们试图做的是回到早期马克思，即黑格尔式的马克思，他强调自由和异化的问题，从社会强加给我们的世界观上的蒙蔽中解放出来。因此，特别是在第一次世界大战和布尔什维克革命之后，有一种趋势是使马克思主义减少实证主义和科学主义，拒绝晚期马克思而支持早期马克思。这不仅适用于法兰克福学派，也适用于葛兰西和卢卡奇。

Now, the main theme of the Frankfurt School is that freedom is reason. In other words, the truth shall make you free. Do you know the passage from the Bible? It's one of the best lines in the Bible. Well, moving from the truth shall make you free to rationality is freedom is something along those lines. And the point is this, rationality is substantive, not merely formal. You can see why they're going to have tremendous disputes with the logical positivists of the Vienna Circle. They're trying to reduce all of thinking, all of reasoning, to some logical formalism. I mean, they reach the end of the line when they get to Gödel's incompleteness theorem, but clearly the goal of the Vienna Circle is to reduce mathematics to logic and logic to a certain set of formal relations.

Well, what the Frankfurt School is saying is that the whole idea of treating reason as purely formal is a mistake. Reason is substantive. That's connected with the idea of it being teleological. It tells you not just how things are, but how things ought to be. Their objection to the Vienna Circle is exactly the kind of objection that Kant would have raised to Hume. They would both say, you Englishmen, or you Anglo-Americans, don't understand the nature of reason. You're using a truncated conception of reason, which prevents the investigation of ends rather than means. And that you buy into a particular kind of ontology and a particular kind of epistemology that is nothing but the illusions of your society. We are trying to break the bounds of those illusions.

法兰克福学派所主张的是，将理性纯粹视为形式性的整个观念是一个错误。理性是实质性的。这与理性具有目的论性质的观点相关联。理性不仅告诉你事物是怎样的，还告诉你事物应当怎样。他们对维也纳学派的反对，恰恰是康德可能会对休谟提出的那种反对。他们都会说，你们英国人，或你们英美人士，不理解理性的本质。你们使用的是被截断的理性概念，这阻碍了对目的而非手段的探究。而且你们接受了一种特定的本体论和认识论，它们不过是你们社会的幻象。我们正试图打破这些幻象的界限。

### 6. Marcuse's Critique of America (马尔库塞对美国的批判)

So the idea that there's no skepticism about is and ought, that reason is normative. Freedom that the Frankfurt School offers us is freedom from the socially imposed bondage of the necessary illusions generated by the society that we were socialized into. They're trying to bootstrap themselves up and out of that.

Now, let's look at some of the leading figures. I've already mentioned Herbert Marcuse. He's probably the most famous and most influential of the members of the Frankfurt School. Because we haven't had the global proletarian revolution, we are necessarily under the realm of ideological illusion.

That a man is never so provincial as when he begins to legislate for the universe.
