### 7. Impressions of Young Chinese Audiences and Family Reflections (对中国年轻听众的印象与家庭反思)

When I returned back to China, I was impressed by all of the young people. Most of those who attended were between 20 and 25 years old. It's very unlike Europe where the average age is around 60. The young people in China are so open-minded. I think they are the hope for China's future.

当我回到中国时，所有年轻人都给我留下了深刻印象。大多数听众年龄在20到25岁之间。这与欧洲大不相同，那里平均年龄在60岁左右。中国的年轻人思想如此开放。我认为他们是中国未来的希望。

Please line up! This is Bach, after all! When this tour is over I need to rest for a while. If you need some rest, listen to your recording of the Goldberg Variations. Seriously, whenever I hear it I feel calm within. It should be sold at pharmacies! My mother would have died of happiness if she had seen this performance. My parents are no longer alive But my four sisters are still there. And the most important member of the family: the piano.

请排队！这毕竟是巴赫！这次巡演结束后我需要休息一段时间。如果你需要休息，听听你的《哥德堡变奏曲》录音。说真的，每当我听到它，内心就感到平静。它应该在药店出售！如果我母亲看到这场演出，她会高兴死的。我的父母已经不在人世了，但我的四个姐妹还在。还有家庭中最重要的成员：钢琴。

It even followed me to the labor camp and later back to Beijing. It is the patriarch of our family. We need to repair it! But someone told us it wouldn't be the same anymore. We have to replace the felt pads. Yes, precisely. When my mother was young, my grandfather didn't let her play the piano. So she invested all of her hopes and dreams in me. I was to realize her dream of playing the piano. She invested all of her energy and did everything she could.

它甚至跟着我去了劳改营，后来又回到了北京。它是我们家的家长。我们需要修理它！但有人告诉我们它不会再像以前一样了。我们必须更换毛毡垫。是的，正是如此。当我母亲年轻时，我祖父不让她弹钢琴。所以她把她所有的希望和梦想都寄托在我身上。我要实现她弹钢琴的梦想。她投入了所有的精力，做了她能做的一切。

In Beijing, during the Cultural Revolution. She was afraid people would say: “She is playing the large western drum.” Every evening, even during the winter, she would wrap herself in a blanket, and would stand guard outside to make sure nobody would hear me playing. When I tried playing on the piano I heard my mother playing Schumann's “Träumerei” for me as a child; I broke into tears. Right to the very end of her life she encouraged me to follow my own path.

在北京，在文化大革命期间。她害怕人们会说：“她在弹西洋大鼓。”每天晚上，即使在冬天，她都会裹着毯子，站在外面确保没有人听到我弹琴。当我试着在钢琴上弹奏时，我听到母亲在我小时候为我弹奏舒曼的《梦幻曲》；我泪流满面。直到她生命的最后一刻，她都鼓励我走自己的路。

### 8. Revisiting the Conservatory's Bittersweet Memories (重访音乐学院的苦乐参半的回忆)

I grew up in this conservatory of music. I spent more time here than at home. I studied from when I was 10 until I was 30. I had the happiest years of my life here. I could study here together with my teachers and classmates. But I also had the most excruciating years of my life here, with public denunciations and accusations. And often, they were extremely cruel. When I was twelve I was publicly denounced here in front of 400 people, the entire conservatory. I will never forget that my entire life.

我在这所音乐学院长大。我在这里度过的时间比在家还多。我从10岁学到30岁。我在这里度过了我一生中最快乐的时光。我可以和我的老师和同学们一起在这里学习。但我在这里也度过了我一生中最痛苦的岁月，经历了公开的批斗和指控。而且，它们常常极其残酷。我十二岁时在这里被公开批斗，面对400人，整个音乐学院。我一生都不会忘记。

During the Cultural Revolution there was a stage here, I remember. Hearings were held here. Yes, yes - right here.

在文化大革命期间，我记得这里有一个舞台。听证会在这里举行。是的，是的——就在这里。

Returning back to the conservatory, my classmate Zuo Yin was excited. But I felt anxious. It wasn't just a place to study, a place for academic discussion and a place to listen to concerts. It was also the location for the Cultural Revolution – for agitation and indoctrination and for denunciations. The bittersweet memories all came back; an emotional roller coaster. Nothing has changed here. It looks just like back then. Yes, that's true.

回到音乐学院，我的同学左因很兴奋。但我感到焦虑。这里不仅仅是一个学习的地方，一个学术讨论和听音乐会的地方。它也是文化大革命的地点——用于鼓动、灌输和批斗。苦乐参半的回忆全都涌上心头；情绪如过山车般起伏。这里什么都没变。看起来和那时一模一样。是的，确实如此。

Do you still remember everything that happened here? During the Cultural Revolution, all concerts as well as public denunciations and punishments were held here. It's hard to believe… I would raise my hands until they hurt, shouting: 'Long live Chairman Mao! Long live Chairman Mao!' There are scenes from the Cultural Revolution that I can't get out of my mind.

你还记得这里发生的一切吗？在文化大革命期间，所有的音乐会以及公开的批斗和惩罚都在这里举行。难以置信……我会举起手直到疼痛，高喊：“毛主席万岁！毛主席万岁！”有些文化大革命的场景我无法从脑海中抹去。

Society should never forget the importance of culture, music and education. They are the most important criteria for an intact and harmonious society. But how does one ensure that such tragedies of the past do not happen again? Sadly, there is no accountability for the past. No one has the courage to confront this tragedy fully. The period is left as a blank in the history of China.

社会永远不应忘记文化、音乐和教育的重要性。它们是完整和谐的社会最重要的标准。但如何确保过去的悲剧不再重演？遗憾的是，对过去没有问责。没有人有勇气完全面对这场悲剧。这段时期在中国历史上留下了一片空白。

### 9. The Beijing Concert: Honoring Gu Shengying and Her Generation (北京音乐会：致敬顾圣婴及其一代人)

The concert in Beijing was one of the toughest challenges for me. I really wanted to play in this concert hall. It is where I last heard Gu Shengying play. That has meant a lot to me. Why is this chair in the way? I am performing to let everyone know that our generation still exists. It had to suffer greatly, not just physical suffering.

北京的音乐会对我来说是最艰难的挑战之一。我非常想在这个音乐厅演奏。这是我最后一次听到顾圣婴演奏的地方。这对我来说意义重大。为什么这把椅子挡着路？我演奏是为了让每个人都知道我们这一代人仍然存在。它遭受了巨大的苦难，不仅仅是身体上的。

but through the lack of culture. There was no music to listen to and even dictionaries had to be copied by hand! I want to show everyone that my generation has not just disappeared. I want to honor my generation. I am so happy that I could share this moment with you.

还有文化的匮乏。没有音乐可听，甚至字典都得手抄！我想向所有人展示，我们这一代人并没有消失。我想向我的同代人致敬。我很高兴能与你分享这一刻。

The last time I heard a concert in this hall it was a concert given by Gu Shengying (Cheng-Ying Kou). I still can remember it very clearly; she played Chopin's "Scherzo". Even after so many years I think we shouldn't forget how she opened up a path for us that she couldn't complete. I am continuing that path for her. Thank you, thank you, thank you!

我最后一次在这个大厅听音乐会，是顾圣婴（Cheng-Ying Kou）的演奏。我仍然记得很清楚；她演奏了肖邦的《谐谑曲》。即使这么多年过去了，我认为我们不应该忘记她如何为我们开辟了一条她未能完成的道路。我正在为她继续这条道路。谢谢，谢谢，谢谢！

### 10. Gratitude, Fulfilled Dreams, and the Role of a Servant (感恩、实现的梦想与仆人的角色)

You played wonderfully! You also came! We haven seen each other for many years! Professor Zhou! My teacher, Zhou Guangren, also came to the concert. She supported me greatly back then. I was her graduate student in 1980 but I immediately decided to go to America. I feared she would be very disappointed. I never thought she would still support me. However, she came up to me and said: "Xiao-Mei, young people like you should go out and see the world. It's a good thing to do!" I have never forgotten this.

你演奏得太棒了！你也来了！我们好多年没见了！周教授！我的老师周广仁也来听音乐会了。她当年非常支持我。1980年我是她的研究生，但我很快就决定去美国。我担心她会非常失望。我没想到她仍然支持我。然而，她走到我面前说：“晓梅，像你这样的年轻人应该出去看看世界。这是件好事！”我从未忘记这一点。

I always dreamed of visiting Bach's grave. And when that dream finally came true, I had a greater dream: I wanted to play his masterpiece in front of his grave. This dream was fulfilled. I have no more wishes. For my whole life I have felt like a puppy whose tail is between her legs. To suddenly become a big star feels uncomfortable. I am a pianist who lacks self-confidence. I have never been self-confident;

我一直梦想着去巴赫的墓前凭吊。当那个梦想终于实现时，我有了一个更大的梦想：我想在他的墓前演奏他的杰作。这个梦想也实现了。我再无他求。我一生都觉得自己像一只夹着尾巴的小狗。突然成为大明星让我感到不自在。我是一个缺乏自信的钢琴家。我从来没有自信过；

I have never understood the feeling of having confidence in myself. That has to do with my upbringing and with the Cultural Revolution that is deeply a part of me.

我从未体会过对自己有信心的感觉。这与我的成长经历以及深深烙印在我身上的文化大革命有关。

I never see myself in the lead role. I always play the role of a servant: a servant to the music, a servant to the composers, a servant to my students. This is my traditional Chinese upbringing.

我从不把自己看作主角。我总是扮演仆人的角色：音乐的仆人，作曲家的仆人，学生的仆人。这是我传统的中国式教养。
