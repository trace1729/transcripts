### 6. Cognitive restructuring: measuring thoughts and thought records (认知重构：测量思维与思维记录)

Now recall the four stages of CBT for depression. So first was education and data collection, followed by the second stage of behavioral activation. So we're now in the cognitive restructuring or the third stage of CBT for depression. Remember, we want to look at these constellation of cognitions which tend to cluster around negative thoughts about the self. I'm unlovable. I'm unworthy. I'm a failure. Negative thoughts about others. No one cares about you. People will hurt you and exploit you. Negative thoughts about the world. It's a very cold and caring, dangerous, or even violent place.

现在回顾一下抑郁症认知行为疗法的四个阶段。首先是教育和数据收集，接着是第二阶段的行动激活。所以我们现在处于认知重构阶段，即抑郁症认知行为疗法的第三阶段。记住，我们要关注那些往往围绕关于自我的消极想法而聚集的认知群。例如：我不讨人喜欢，我没有价值，我是个失败者。关于他人的消极想法：没人关心你，人们会伤害你、利用你。关于世界的消极想法：这是一个非常冷漠、危险甚至暴力的地方。

### 7. Comparing medication, CBT, and combined treatment outcomes (比较药物、认知行为疗法及联合治疗的结果)

Folks often ask which is better, medications or CBT? Partly a personal choice, but the bottom line is that medications and CBT are equally effective for mild to moderate depression at the end of treatment and at a six-month follow-up. But if you go further out, it looks like CBT is superior to medications unless a person wants to be medicated for the remainder of their lives. And why might that be? If you think about CBT, remember my goal is to help you to be your own CBT therapist, to give you a toolbox of interventions that you can pull out and use anytime you want. So when you finish CBT for depression, you've ended your connection with me, but you still have that toolkit and you can keep dosing yourself essentially with those interventions.

人们经常问哪种更好，药物还是认知行为疗法？部分是个人的选择，但底线是，对于轻度至中度抑郁症，在治疗结束时和六个月随访时，药物和认知行为疗法同样有效。但如果再往后看，认知行为疗法似乎优于药物，除非一个人愿意在余生中一直服药。为什么会这样呢？如果你想想认知行为疗法，记住我的目标是帮助你成为自己的认知行为疗法治疗师，给你一个装满干预措施的工具箱，你可以随时拿出来使用。因此，当你完成抑郁症的认知行为疗法后，你与我的联系结束了，但你仍然拥有那个工具箱，你可以基本上继续用那些干预措施给自己“用药”。

In a study done by Rob DeRubis and Steve Holland in 2005, they did a randomized controlled trial of cognitive therapy versus pharmacotherapy in moderate to severe depression. They had 24 sessions delivered by experienced therapists, and they found that cognitive therapy was as efficacious as pharmacotherapy. Now, this is notable in that it used to be the assumption that if someone was severely depressed, you had to break out pharmacotherapy and you couldn't do cognitive therapy, and that just doesn't seem to be the case.

在2005年Rob DeRubis和Steve Holland进行的一项研究中，他们对中重度抑郁症进行了认知疗法与药物治疗的随机对照试验。他们由经验丰富的治疗师提供了24次治疗，结果发现认知疗法与药物治疗同样有效。值得注意的是，过去人们通常认为，如果某人患有严重抑郁症，就必须采用药物治疗，而不能进行认知疗法，但事实似乎并非如此。

In a similar study by Siegel, Vincent, and Levent in 2002, they looked at combination therapy, and they found that combination therapy of medications and cognitive therapy was probably superior to either alone. End of the story is, you could use either. When more severe cases, I would probably recommend a combination, but again, depending on patient preferences.

在Siegel、Vincent和Levent于2002年进行的一项类似研究中，他们考察了联合治疗，发现药物与认知疗法的联合治疗可能优于单独使用任何一种疗法。总而言之，你可以选择其中任何一种。对于更严重的病例，我可能会推荐联合治疗，但同样，这取决于患者的偏好。
