### 4. Core Principles of CBT (CBT的核心原则)

I think, though, as we learn about CBT, it's important to keep in mind what I see as some of the special features of CBT in comparison to other kinds of psychotherapy. First, CBT is collaborative and transparent. And by collaborative, I mean that the patient and the therapist join together in a partnership. They roll up their sleeves together, begin collecting data on the problem from the individual's real-world situations. They create a formulation, and then they develop a treatment plan together. It's all done together as a team.

不过，我想，当我们学习CBT时，重要的是要记住我所认为的CBT与其他心理治疗相比的一些特殊之处。首先，CBT是协作性和透明的。所谓协作性，我指的是患者和治疗师结成伙伴关系。他们一起卷起袖子，开始从个体的现实生活情境中收集关于问题的数据。他们共同创建一份案例概念化，然后一起制定治疗计划。这一切都是作为一个团队共同完成的。

It's transparent because all of those steps and the rationale for all of those steps is made explicit. There's no magic happening behind the screen. It's all above board. It's all on the table. Remember our goal as a CBT therapist is to teach a patient to be his or her own CBT therapist. So you need to know how it works and why we did the things that we did for your treatment plan.

它是透明的，因为所有这些步骤及其理由都是明确说明的。幕后没有魔法。一切都摆在桌面上。记住，作为CBT治疗师，我们的目标是教会患者成为自己的CBT治疗师。所以，你需要知道它是如何运作的，以及我们为什么为你的治疗计划做我们所做的事情。

The second is that CBT is empirical. And here I don't just mean psychotherapy research and evidence about whether or not it works. They have coined a term called collaborative empiricism. Now we've talked about the collaborative part, but the empirical part is that we'll come up with a hypothesis, an idea of what makes Michael angry or what makes Maria depressed or what makes Carol socially anxious. But it's only a hypothesis. We then have to test out that hypothesis in the real world, usually by giving homework assignments. So the patient will have their hypothesis, they'll have their homework assignment, then go out to test it out to see if it works. If it works, great, we'll go to the next step. But if it doesn't work, again, it's grist for the mill. We'll roll up our sleeves, we'll figure out what happened, and we'll come up with the next hypothesis or the next homework assignment.

第二，CBT是经验性的。在这里，我不仅仅指心理治疗研究及其是否有效的证据。他们创造了一个术语叫协作经验主义。我们已经讨论了协作部分，但经验部分在于，我们会提出一个假设，一个关于什么让迈克尔生气、什么让玛丽亚抑郁或什么让卡罗尔社交焦虑的想法。但这只是一个假设。然后我们必须在现实世界中检验这个假设，通常是通过布置家庭作业。所以，患者会有他们的假设，他们会有家庭作业，然后出去检验它是否有效。如果有效，很好，我们进入下一步。但如果无效，那又是磨坊的谷物（有用的素材）。我们会卷起袖子，弄清楚发生了什么，然后提出下一个假设或下一个家庭作业。

The next feature is that CBT is time limited. There is an endpoint. Now it varies depending on the individual, sometimes on the insurance company, but it usually ranges from between 12 to say 24 sessions, depending again on resources severity and what the treatment goals might be. It is skills focused. Again, no magic behind the curtain. We're going to teach an individual skills that they can practice in the real world, come back and talk about obstacles or challenges. They will hone those skills and continue practicing until they feel that they have mastered those skills.

下一个特点是CBT是有时间限制的。有一个终点。现在这因个体而异，有时取决于保险公司，但通常范围在12到24次会谈之间，再次取决于资源、严重程度以及治疗目标可能是什么。它以技能为中心。再次强调，幕后没有魔法。我们将教授个体可以在现实世界中练习的技能，回来讨论障碍或挑战。他们将磨练这些技能并继续练习，直到他们觉得自己掌握了这些技能。

It's also symptom focused. So there's oftentimes a number of different symptom measures for depression, for anxiety, for positive emotion, for whatever the issue might be. We'll want to get a baseline, but then we'll want to reassess that individual over time as they're using those skills to see if those scores, those symptoms, are improving.

它也是以症状为中心的。所以，通常有许多不同的症状测量方法，用于抑郁、焦虑、积极情绪，或无论问题是什么。我们想要获得一个基线，但随后我们想要随着个体使用这些技能而随时间重新评估他们，看看那些分数、那些症状是否在改善。

CBT is also focused on the present. So we won't sit someone down and say, tell me about your childhood. Tell me about your relationship with your parents.
